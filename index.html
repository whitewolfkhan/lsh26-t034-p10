The tariff this tool uses, and nothing else

How the rules were read

  1. The slab counter is the calendar month's running unit total. It resets on the 1st. A recharge never resets it.
  2. Slabs price unit positions, so unit 75 of a month costs 4.63 and unit 76 costs 5.26. A day that crosses a boundary is split across the two rates.
  3. The 42 tk demand charge and 40 tk meter rent are taken together, once, at the first recharge of a calendar month. A month with no recharge in it carries neither.
  4. VAT is 5% of the energy amount, as written. It is not charged on the demand charge or the meter rent.
  5. A recharge is credited at the start of its day; that day's units are then billed.
  6. Cost means what the meter consumes — energy, VAT and the fixed charges that actually fall due — not the amount deposited.
